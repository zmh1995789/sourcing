FAQ

What quantities can you handle?

Any, honestly assessed. 1688 suppliers often accept small runs; Alibaba factories commonly want 300–1,000+ units. Tell me your target quantity and I'll tell you upfront whether it's realistic for your product.

Do I have to use Trade Assurance?

Trade Assurance exists only on Alibaba.com. For Alibaba orders I strongly recommend it, with one condition: your spec has to be written into the order text, because that written order is the only version a dispute is judged against — getting it in there is part of my verification report. 1688 has no Trade Assurance at all: orders run through my 1688 account (1688 settles via Alipay and doesn't take international cards), so the dispute rights are mine to exercise on your behalf, backed by the verification report, the sample record, and the milestone evidence I collect. Before any 1688 payment, you get the plan in writing: what is paid, at which node, and what evidence must exist first — plus the risks that no arrangement can remove, stated upfront.

Can you arrange shipping to my country?

I coordinate with your freight forwarder on the China side — pickup, carton specs, documents. If you don't have one, I can introduce forwarders I've seen perform well. The shipping contract and payment stay between you and them.

What does the inspection cover?

An independent inspector checks quantity, workmanship, labeling, and packing against your spec before you release final payment. Typical cost is $100–$300, paid by you directly to the inspection company.

What if I already have a supplier?

I can verify them, re-negotiate, review their sample documentation, and monitor production. Plenty of clients start exactly there.

Do you guarantee savings?

No — and be wary of anyone who does. Prices depend on your spec, quantity, and the market that week. What I guarantee is verified suppliers, genuine negotiation, and complete visibility into every number.
