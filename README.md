# sourcing

China sourcing agent — landing page

This repository hosts the landing page for a China-based sourcing agent (`index.html`). It covers supplier verification on 1688, Alibaba and Taobao, Mandarin negotiation, sample checks and independent inspection. The service is fee-based, with no supplier commissions.

Live page: https://zmh1995789.github.io/sourcing/

## Free Quickstart

1. Open the live landing page above, or open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Send a product link or specification, your target price and quantity, and your delivery address and timeline, using the contact form on the page or by email to zmh1995456@gmail.com.
3. You will receive a reply within one business day with a feasibility read before you pay anything.
4. If the product is feasible, choose a service tier on the landing page. If it is not feasible, you will be told so.
